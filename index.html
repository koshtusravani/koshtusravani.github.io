Clinical Dx Agent

AI agent
Shipped

A clinical decision support agent that reasons through a diagnosis step by step, calling tools as it goes.

  • Two phase ReAct agent (adaptive diagnostic loop and treatment safety loop) with Claude tool use and dynamic step budgeting, with zero unhandled failures.
  • Six category failure taxonomy with automatic retry and backoff, plus a deterministic safety net that overrides the model on severe drug interaction risks.
  • 95% correct on UTI and zero incorrect Type 2 Diabetes diagnoses, with 43% of ambiguous cases escalated rather than guessed.

Evaluated on a 100 case synthetic Synthea suite across 5 conditions, averaging $0.19 and 36.5 seconds per case.
