Amicus

Shipped

A retrieval augmented generation system that answers questions about US Supreme Court opinions with citations a lawyer can verify.

  • Cut fabricated citations from 93% to 0% against a no retrieval baseline, and refused every out of corpus query.
  • Added cross encoder reranking (bge-reranker-v2-m3), lifting Recall@3 from 83.3% to 96.7% and MRR from 0.819 to 0.900.
  • Fixed a citation normalization mismatch that inflated fabrication metrics by 43 points and a retrieval truncation bug that dropped relevant case law.
  • Now extending it into a controlled study: Citation Faithfulness Under Retrieval Failure: A Controlled Study of Grounded Legal RAG.

Clinical Dx Agent

Shipped

A clinical decision support agent that reasons through a diagnosis step by step, calling tools as it goes.

  • Two phase ReAct agent (adaptive diagnostic loop and treatment safety loop) with Claude tool use and dynamic step budgeting, with zero unhandled failures.
  • Six category failure taxonomy with automatic retry and backoff, plus a deterministic safety net that overrides the model on severe drug interaction risks.
  • 95% correct on UTI and zero incorrect Type 2 Diabetes diagnoses, with 43% of ambiguous cases escalated rather than guessed.

Evaluated on a 100 case synthetic Synthea suite across 5 conditions, averaging $0.19 and 36.5 seconds per case.
